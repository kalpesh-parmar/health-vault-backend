<task>
<name>Task 1: Implement 10-Step Pipeline Trace Logging in chat.service.js</name>
<action>
1. Instrument `sendMessage()` and its lifecycle methods in `chat.service.js` with structured logging under `CHAT_LIFECYCLE_TRACE`:
   - Step 1: Input receipt & sanitization (`question`, `userId`, `sessionId`).
   - Step 2: Language detection (`detectedLanguage`, `passedLang`).
   - Step 3: Early translation (`englishQuestion`).
   - Step 4: Intercept evaluation (`isReminderQuery`, `isMedicationListRequest`, `isProfileQuestion`).
   - Step 5: Intent analysis (`intent`, `documentScope`, `intentReason`).
   - Step 6: Context domain resolution (`detectedDomains`).
   - Step 7: Database queries executed & records retrieved (medications count, occurrences count: taken, missed, pending).
   - Step 8: Context string generated (`patientContextStr` preview).
   - Step 9: LLM Prompt and raw answer (`model`, `generationDurationMs`, `answerPreview`).
   - Step 10: Final reply delivery (`mode`, `emergency`, `replyLength`).
2. Specifically in `_handleReminderIntercept()`:
   - Log warning when `occurrences.length === 0` and it falls back to `medicationRepository.findAll()`.
   - Log breakdown of occurrences (`total`, `taken`, `missed`, `pending`).
</action>
