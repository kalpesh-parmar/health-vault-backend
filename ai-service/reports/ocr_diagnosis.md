## Step 3 — Instrumentation Applied

In accordance with constraints, **only timing instrumentation** was added to `ai-service/app/services/pipeline/ocr_stage.py`. Zero logic, zero parameters, and zero models were altered.

A lightweight context-manager `ocr_timer` was added at [`ocr_stage.py:38-47`](file:///d:/TECHROVER/health-vault/health-vault-backend/ai-service/app/services/pipeline/ocr_stage.py#L38-L47):

```python
@contextlib.contextmanager
def ocr_timer(step_name: str, **extra: Any):
    t_start = time.monotonic()
    result = {"elapsed_ms": 0}
    try:
        yield result
    finally:
        result["elapsed_ms"] = int((time.monotonic() - t_start) * 1000)
        extra_str = f" ({', '.join(f'{k}={v}' for k, v in extra.items())})" if extra else ""
        logger.info("%s: %d ms%s", step_name, result["elapsed_ms"], extra_str)
```
